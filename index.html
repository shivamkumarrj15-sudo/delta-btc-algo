📚 Crypto Options Trading Kaise Kaam Karta Hai? (Guide)
1. Options Trading Kya Hai?
Options ek aisa contract hai jisme aap kam paise (Premium) dekar Bitcoin ke upar ya niche jane par trade kar sakte hain.
2. CALL Option (CE) - Teji / Bullish
• **Kab Buy Karein?** Jab aapko lagta hai BTC ka price upar jayega.
• **Risk:** Sirf utna jitna aapne Premium pay kiya (Limited Risk).
• **Profit:** BTC jitna upar jayega, utna bada Unlimited Profit 🚀
3. PUT Option (PE) - Mandi / Bearish
• **Kab Buy Karein?** Jab aapko lagta hai BTC ka price niche girega.
• **Risk:** Limited to Premium paid.
• **Profit:** BTC jitna niche girega, utna bada Profit!
4. ATM, ITM, OTM Strike Price Kya Hai?
• ATM (At The Money): Current BTC price ke sabse paas wala strike (e.g. BTC $84,150 par hai toh $84,000 ATM hai).
• ITM (In The Money): Jisme intrinsic value pehle se hoti hai (Surakshit option, Delta high).
• OTM (Out of The Money): Saste options. BTC tezi se bhaga toh 5x-10x return, par expiry par zero bhi ho sakte hain.
5. Option Greeks (Delta & Theta)
• Delta: BTC $1,000 move karega toh option kitna dollar badhega (e.g. 0.50 Delta = $500 profit).
• Theta Decay: Roz time bitne par option ka premium thoda ghisata hai (Time Value).
